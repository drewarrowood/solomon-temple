    How to read this

    Cubits are drawn at 0.5 m. The house is 60 × 20 × 30 cubits; the inner sanctuary is a 20-cubit cube. Side chambers step 5 / 6 / 7 cubits high and wide.

    Furnishings are stylized stand-ins (ark, cherubim, lampstands, showbread, incense altar, bronze sea and altar) so the plan can be orbited and studied.
